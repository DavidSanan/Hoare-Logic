How Hoare logic works

Hoare logic proves that a program meets its specification, without running it. This page explains what a specification says, how the rules of the logic are used, and how the weakest precondition calculus organises a proof.

Weakest precondition calculator

Type a loop-free program and a postcondition. The calculator applies the rules backwards, one statement at a time, and shows every intermediate assertion. If you also give a precondition, it tests the verification condition P ⇒ wp on sample states.
